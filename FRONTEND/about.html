About MedVerify

MedVerify is India's first AI-powered medicine verification system. Using QR code scanning and blockchain technology, we help you ensure the medicines you take are genuine and safe.

Key Features

  • ✅ Instant QR code scanning
  • ✅ Real-time medicine verification
  • ✅ Expiry date tracking
  • ✅ Report fake medicines
  • ✅ Scan history
